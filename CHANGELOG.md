# Hearthside

## 0.3.0-beta.3

*(Français plus bas.)*

### Your corpse on the map (new)
- Once you release your spirit, your corpse shows on the Hearthside map, as on the game's own map.
- The line at the bottom right of the map says how far it lies and in which direction, to run back to it.
- It goes away as soon as you are back to life.

### Fixed
- Parcours: someone joining your group could show as "Unknown" or at level 0. Parcours now waits until the game knows them, then shows their real name, class and level. Joins already saved as "Unknown" are hidden.

### Good to know
- Something breaks? Tell me on Discord, with a screenshot.

## 0.3.0-beta.2

*(Français plus bas.)*

### Guild and friends on the map (new)
- Everyone online in your guild and on your friends list (Battle.net friends playing Forever too) shows by zone: a badge with how many are there on a continent map, in a corner of the zone's own map. Hover it for names and levels. Nobody needs to install anything for this.
- Players who share where they are show as a dot in their class colour, where they stand: name, level, class and distance on hover.
- Hearthside speaks the same open format as OpenMap and also listens to OneForAll: players of those addons and of Hearthside see each other.
- The first time you open the map, Hearthside asks once whether to share your place. Only your place is sent, never from a dungeon, raid or battleground. Change it any time in **Settings → Map**.
- Your group stays drawn by the game, as before. Streamer mode shows nobody.
- The map's menu has a new line, "Guild and friends", to hide them.

### Good to know
- Close the game, update the addon, then start the game again: this version adds a file.
- If Hearthside opens on the game screen instead of the second screen after the update, type /reload once.
- Something breaks? Tell me on Discord, with a screenshot.

## 0.3.0-beta.1

*(Français plus bas.)*

### Parcours, the page of the day (new)
The Parcours tab now writes your day while you play, on the second screen.
- Newest first, with the time: logins and breaks, arrivals and first visits, quests turned in, levels, deaths with what probably killed you, enemies defeated (grouped by place), players joining your group, and tales of the Ink you finish.
- A flight counts once: one arrival when you land, with where you flew from.
- A dungeon is one block: its bosses as round portraits, the ones not beaten yet greyed out. Open it to see the chosen boss in 3D.
- On the right, the day in numbers: play time, experience, quests, enemies, bosses, deaths, and who played with you.
- A banner with the map of your zone and your character, who cheers when you gain a level.
- Short animations only when something happens while the page is open. **Settings**: "Reduce motion" and "Show 3D models".
- The days are kept for two weeks. The page starts with this version: earlier days stay empty.

### Dungeons on the map (new)
- Inside a dungeon, the map shows its plan, floor by floor, with your quests there and its bosses beside it.
- Before going in: click a dungeon's entrance on the map to see its plan, the quests to pick up first and its bosses.

### My characters (new)
In the Inventory tab: each of your characters as it was at its last logout — where, gold, rested experience, free bag slots, professions and their cooldowns.

### What I missed (new)
Back from being away, on a flight or out of a long fight: a short band says what happened meanwhile (whispers, mail, an auction sold, a cooldown ready). It only shows when something happened. It can be turned off in Settings.

### Fixed
- Logging in with a dead character no longer counts one more death.

### Good to know
- Works with the current Hearthside Companion (0.2.0 Beta): nothing to update there.
- Close the game, update the addon, then start the game again: this version adds files.
- If Hearthside opens on the game screen instead of the second screen after the update, type /reload once.
- Something breaks? Tell me on Discord, with a screenshot.

- Une bannière avec la carte de votre zone et votre personnage, qui acclame quand vous gagnez un niveau.

- Avant d'y aller : cliquez sur l'entrée d'un donjon sur la carte pour voir son plan, les quêtes à prendre avant d'entrer et ses boss.

Dans l'onglet Inventaire : chacun de vos personnages tel qu'à sa dernière déconnexion — où, or, repos, places libres dans les sacs, métiers et leurs temps de recharge.

### Corrigé
- Se connecter avec un personnage mort ne compte plus une mort de trop.

## 0.2.0-beta.2

*(Français plus bas.)*

### Ink of the Realms (new)
Write tales hidden in Azeroth, and follow those of other players, from a new **Ink** tab.
- Three kinds of tale: **hidden notes** to search for, a **treasure hunt**, or a **quest** with its objectives on the map.
- A page opens when you reach its place, carry its item, answer its riddle, talk to a character, do an emote or slay creatures. The author can also ask for a level or an hour of the day.
- Write in the workshop, try your tale yourself, then seal it: the seal is a short text to paste on Discord, a forum or in a whisper.
- **Share in the game**: offer a tale to a player who is online and has this version. They accept or refuse. *This part is new and has not been tried widely yet: if it fails, copy the seal instead, and tell me on Discord.*
- Your tales show on the map, in the Quests tab and in Journey.

### Map
- **Icons menu**, top right of the map: tick what you want to see.
- The game's own icons: dungeons, raids, repairs, flight master, innkeeper, your class trainer, bank, auction house and mailboxes (they need the optional QuestieDB addon).
- City mailboxes: neighbours share a pin, and more of them appear as you zoom in.
- Your own marker: Ctrl+right-click places it, Ctrl+right-click on it removes it.
- Zone levels, your coordinates, the cursor's, and the distance between them.
- A quest offered at several places shows the one nearest to you.

### Also
- The HEARTHSIDE name at the top opens its home, with the tales, In My Footsteps and the Contract.
- Texts and layouts that were cut on small or narrow screens.

### Good to know
- Works with the current Hearthside Companion (0.2.0 Beta): nothing to update there.
- Close the game, update the addon, then start the game again: this version adds files.

## 0.2.0-beta.1

First public beta. *(Français plus bas.)*

### What Hearthside does
- **Map**: the World of Warcraft map of your region on your second screen, with its quest markers linked to Hearthside's quest list, and the path you travelled this session.
- **Quests**: your quest log at a glance, quests ready to turn in first. With the optional QuestieDB addon, the quests you can pick up appear on the map, sorted by level.
- **Journey**: the milestones of your adventure, region by region.
- **Discussion**: the game's chat on the second screen, with your own tabs.
- **Community** (experimental, off by default): prepares a question in your chat line when a group quest shows up. You choose the channel and send it yourself.
- On a single screen, a button on the minimap opens or closes the Hearthside window, which fades while you move.

### New since the tester betas
- Discussion receives every kind of message the game's chat shows: professions, loot, money, experience, reputation, achievements, NPCs.
- Make your own tabs (up to 8) in Settings > Discussion, or right-click a tab. The first tab starts from your game's chat settings.
- Copy a line (click its time or dot) or a whole tab, open web addresses in a copy box, and search the history.
- Optional: keep the last 200 lines after a reload, per character, on your computer only. Off by default.
- Right-click a player's name in Discussion: whisper, invite, add as friend, ignore, copy the name, or "Who is it?" (level, class and zone, answered in the chat).
- A sound when a whisper arrives, from the game or a Battle.net friend, heard even with effects muted. Choose it, or turn it off, in Settings > Discussion.
- The status bar names your durability and shows money with coin icons.

### Good to know
- For two screens, install the Hearthside Companion (Windows) from its releases page, and start it before WoW.
- Testers: Hearthside was called Azeroth Journey during the private betas. Install the new Companion first: it carries your settings, history and journey over to the new name.
- World of Warcraft Forever 1.60.1 (Interface 16001). English and French.

## 0.2.0-beta.1 (français)

Première bêta publique.

### Ce que fait Hearthside
- **Carte** : la carte de World of Warcraft de votre région sur le second écran, avec ses repères de quêtes reliés à la liste de Hearthside, et le chemin parcouru pendant la session.
- **Quêtes** : votre journal d'un coup d'œil, les quêtes prêtes à rendre en premier. Avec l'addon facultatif QuestieDB, les quêtes à prendre apparaissent sur la carte, rangées par niveau.
- **Parcours** : les étapes de votre aventure, région par région.
- **Discussion** : le chat du jeu sur le second écran, avec vos propres onglets.
- **Communauté** (expérimental, désactivé par défaut) : prépare une question dans votre ligne de chat quand une quête de groupe apparaît. Vous choisissez le canal et vous l'envoyez vous-même.
- Sur un seul écran, un bouton sur la minicarte ouvre ou ferme la fenêtre Hearthside, qui devient transparente quand vous bougez.

### Nouveau depuis les bêtas des testeurs
- La Discussion reçoit tous les messages du chat du jeu : métiers, butin, argent, expérience, réputation, hauts faits, PNJ.
- Composez vos onglets (8 au plus) dans Réglages › Discussion, ou par un clic droit sur un onglet. Le premier onglet reprend les réglages de votre chat du jeu.
- Copiez une ligne (clic sur son heure ou son point) ou tout un onglet, ouvrez les adresses web dans une zone de copie, et cherchez dans l'historique.
- Facultatif : garder les 200 dernières lignes après un rechargement, par personnage, sur votre ordinateur uniquement. Désactivé par défaut.
- Clic droit sur le nom d'un joueur dans la Discussion : chuchoter, inviter, ajouter en ami, ignorer, copier le nom, ou « Qui est-ce ? » (niveau, classe et zone, en réponse dans le chat).
- Un son à l'arrivée d'un chuchotement, du jeu ou d'un ami Battle.net, audible même avec les effets coupés. Choisissez-le, ou coupez-le, dans Réglages › Discussion.
- La barre d'état nomme la durabilité et affiche l'argent avec les icônes des pièces.

### À savoir
- Pour deux écrans, installez le Hearthside Companion (Windows) depuis sa page de versions, et lancez-le avant WoW.
- Testeurs : Hearthside s'appelait Azeroth Journey pendant les bêtas privées. Installez d'abord le nouveau Companion : il reporte vos réglages, votre historique et votre parcours sous le nouveau nom.
- World of Warcraft Forever 1.60.1 (Interface 16001). Anglais et français.
