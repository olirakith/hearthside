# Hearthside

A second-screen companion for World of Warcraft Forever: your map, quests and journey live on a
second monitor, while the game keeps the first one. *(Français plus bas.)*

## What it does

- **Map**: your region's map next to the game, with quest markers and the road you travelled.
- **Quests**: your quest log at a glance, quests ready to turn in first.
- **Journey**: the milestones of your adventure.
- **Discussion**: the game's chat on the second screen, with your own tabs, copy and search.
- **Community** (experimental, off by default): prepares a group question in your chat line. You send it yourself.

## Install

| Part | Role | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hearthside addon** | Everything you see in game | CurseForge or Wago app |
| **Hearthside Companion** (Windows) | Places WoW on your game screen and opens Hearthside on the second one | [Companion releases](https://github.com/olirakith/hearthside-companion/releases) |

The Companion moves and resizes the WoW window, as you would by hand, and reads a small signal the
addon draws in a corner of the game window, to know the addon is ready. It does not modify the game,
read anything else on screen or send keys. Start it before WoW. On a single screen, the addon works alone:
a minimap button opens the Hearthside window.

## Privacy

- Everything stays on your computer: no telemetry, no account, no server.
- The chat history is kept after a reload only if you turn it on, and only on your computer.
- Community never sends anything for you.

## Compatibility

World of Warcraft Forever 1.60.1 (Interface 16001), Windows 10 or 11 for the Companion. English and French.

## En français

Hearthside met votre carte, vos quêtes et votre parcours sur un second écran pendant que le jeu
garde le premier. Installez l'addon avec l'appli CurseForge ou Wago, et, pour deux écrans,
